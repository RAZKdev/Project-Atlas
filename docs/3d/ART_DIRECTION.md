# Project Atlas Museum — 3D Art Direction

## 1. Aesthetic Identity
**Premium Stylized Realism / Museum Architectural Visualization / Cinematic Personal Archive**

Project Atlas is not a game. It is a quiet personal digital museum of authentic memories and cartographic discovery.
The 3D environment serves as a serene, contemplative spatial extension of the personal archive.

## 2. World-Scale Contract
* **Canonical Scale:** 1 Three.js unit = 1.0 meter.
* **Human Reference:** Explorer height = 1.70 m, Eye level = 1.60 m, Collision radius = 0.28 m.
* **Architectural Pavilion:** 6.00 m width × 8.00 m length × 3.50 m wall height with 0.50 m raised stylobate foundation.
* **Doorway Clearance:** 1.80 m width × 2.40 m height.

## 3. Materials & Texture Palette (PBR)
* **Limestone / Travertine (`0xf6f1e8`, roughness 0.82):** Matte natural stone for pavilion exterior walls and pilasters.
* **Architectural Walnut (`0x3d2b1a`, roughness 0.55):** Deep lustrous wood for reveals, door frames, baseboards, and furniture.
* **Standing-Seam Zinc Slate (`0x2d2a27`, roughness 0.65, metalness 0.38):** Modern architectural pitched roof with standing seam ribs.
* **Dark Anodized Bronze (`0x262320`, roughness 0.42, metalness 0.80):** Window mullions, door handles, and exterior lantern posts.
* **Burnished Brass (`0xd4a843`, roughness 0.28, metalness 0.92):** Exhibit pedestals, commemorative plaques, and nautical instruments.
* **Transmissive Aquatic Pool (`0x2cb5b5`, roughness 0.08, transmission 0.92):** Double-layer refractive water for the central garden fountain.

## 4. Lighting & Atmosphere
* **Sunlight:** Warm directional sun (`0xfff2dc`, intensity 1.5) angled from the south-east with soft cascaded PCF shadows.
* **Hemisphere Sky Fill:** Natural sky (`0xfff6ea`) and earth bounce (`0x9c8b74`) preventing harsh crushed black shadows.
* **Accent Fixtures:** Warm 2700K recessed ceiling downlights inside the gallery, portico entrance illumination, and subtle underwater fountain glow.
